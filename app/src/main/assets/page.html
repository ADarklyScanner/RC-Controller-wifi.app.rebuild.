Controls

Driving

Extra lock leans the car's own trim into a full turn for a tighter circle (the trick from the stock app). It eases back after the turn, so set it to 0 if the car drifts for a moment afterwards.

Adds a MAX speed: the car's full range, which the stock app only reaches with its speed turned all the way up (speed 3 here is already twice the stock app's normal top speed). Drains the battery faster. Resets to speed 3 every time the app opens.

Tests

Tries the three slots in the drive message the stock app never changes, one value at a time, with the car standing still. Wheels off the ground, then watch the car and the camera and note the value when anything happens. Stop ends a test.

Found so far: Slot A is the car's steering trim (it is the Steering trim setting above). Slot B made the motor sound on one of its values.
